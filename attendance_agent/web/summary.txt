   🚀 Product Overview: CampusOS Attendance AI 
  CampusOS Attendance AI is an intelligent, conversational, and predictive attendance management SaaS platform
  designed for modern higher education institutions and students.

  Instead of forcing students and faculty to navigate clunky, legacy ERP portals, CampusOS pairs live database
  records with an autonomous, guardrailed LangChain AI agent and a real-time mathematical target simulator.

  ## 🧩 6 Core SaaS Modules (What to Show on Screen)
    graph TD
        A[CampusOS Attendance SaaS] --> B[1. Live Dashboard & KPIs]
        A --> C[2. Conversational AI Agent]
        A --> D[3. Interactive Slot Matrix Calendar]
        A --> E[4. Attendance Target Simulator]
        A --> F[5. Faculty & Day Analytics]
        A --> G[6. Audit Trail & CSV Export]

  1. Live Overview Dashboard:
      • Real-time circular radial attendance gauge (e.g., 78.12% Marked Ratio).
      • Quick KPI cards: Present Slots (25), Absent Slots (7), Total Slots (32), Safe Skips Left (1).
      • Instant quick-prompt launcher buttons.
  2. Autonomous Attendance AI Workspace:
      • Multi-turn conversational interface powered by LangChain & LangGraph checkpointer memory.
      • Built-in Date Inspector tool to pick any date and ask questions in 1 click.
      • Strict guardrails preventing off-topic drift; answers queries about dates, monthly aggregates, absent
      history, and professor attendance.
  3. Interactive Calendar & Slot Matrix:
      • Visual calendar grid with attendance color tags (Green for present, Red for absent, Yellow for pending).
      • Side drawer breaking down individual time slots (e.g., 01:00 - 02:00), status, and assigned faculty name.
  4. Predictive Target Simulator & Bunk Planner:
      • Interactive percentage target slider (50% to 98%).
      • Real-time mathematical calculation:
          • Required Consecutive Classes to reach goal.
          • Safe Skips / Bunks Allowed before dropping below the criteria.
      • Standard collegiate milestone table (75%, 80%, 85%, 90%, 95%).
  5. Faculty & Day-of-Week Analytics:
      • Day-of-week attendance distribution bar charts (identifying peak absence days like Mondays/Fridays).
      • Professor-wise breakdown showing attendance rate per faculty member.
      • 100% Present consistency streak tracker.
  6. Audit & Records Explorer:
      • Searchable, filterable table for all recorded sessions.
      • Instant 1-click CSV export for reports and administration.

   💡 Key SaaS Differentiators to Highlight in Marketing 

  1. Ultra-Low Latency LLM Gateway: Groq-accelerated LLaMA 3.3 70B with automatic Gemini 2.5 Flash fallbacks for
  99.9% uptime.
  2. Deterministic Data Grounding: The LLM operates strictly on verified Supabase student tables and custom
  tools (get_attendance_for_date, get_attendance_for_month).
  3. Actionable Predictive Math: Real-time ceiling and floor math for student attendance recovery planning.
  4. Clean Glassmorphic UI: High-contrast, accessibility-first design with responsive desktop and mobile support.
