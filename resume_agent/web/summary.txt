  ### 1. Instant ATS Scanner & Structural Audit

  • Real-time 0–100 ATS Score Gauge with letter grades (A+ to D).
  • Automated checklist: Contact verification, graduation year detection, project count & depth.
  • Identifies missing critical sections (Skills, Projects, Experience, Objective, Education).
  • "Ask AI to Fix" one-click prompt integration.

  ### 2. Semantic JD Matching & Placement Eligibility

  • Side-by-side Job Description input with built-in presets (Full Stack SDE, AI/ML Engineer, Frontend).
  • Automated Eligibility Verdict: Eligible (>=70%), Borderline (40–69%), Not Eligible (<40%).
  • Visual Skill Tags: Green (Matched on Resume) vs Red (Missing / Priority Learning Gap).
  • Comprehensive natural language feedback and recommendations.

  ### 3. The 6 Specialized AI Copilots

  1. Resume Review Agent: In-depth audit and impact-driven bullet point enhancements.
  2. Placement Eligibility Checker: Source-of-truth criteria validation against company requirements.
  3. Skill Gap Analyzer: Prioritized roadmap to close missing technical competencies.
  4. Interview Preparation Studio: Project-grounded technical questions and STAR-method behavioral scenarios.
  5. Coding & DSA Practice Engine: 4-week structured algorithmic mastery roadmap (LeetCode patterns).
  6. Career Trajectory & Role Advisor: Long-term career paths, transitions, and growth analysis.

  ### 4. Multi-Candidate Leaderboard (Enterprise / Recruiter View)

  • Batch upload multiple PDF/DOCX resumes.
  • Instantly ranks all candidates against any target JD or ATS benchmark.
  • Displays coverage percentages, matched vs missing counts, and ranked leaderboard tables.

  ### 5. Enterprise Guardrails & Anti-Hallucination

  • Strict grounding in vector chunks — never invents resume facts.
  • Automatic PII redaction on output to protect candidate privacy.
  • Robust prompt injection mitigation.
