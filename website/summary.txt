  ### 1. 🏢 Multi-Tenant, Role-Based Access Control

  • HOD / Dean Portal: Manage departments, assign class teachers, monitor institutional attendance metrics, and
  dispatch staff invitations.
  • Teacher Portal: Full classroom management, student image verification, dynamic slot-by-slot status updates
  (Present / Absent / Pending), and instant CSV data export.
  • Student Portal: Transparent personal analytics with attendance percentage meters, breakdown of attended
  sessions, and date-wise histories.

  ### 2. 📊 Real-Time Analytics & Smart Defaulter Engine

  • Customizable Defaulter Threshold: Admins/teachers set custom percentage criteria (e.g. 75%), automatically
  flagging at-risk students in real time.
  • Visual progress rings and attendance stats (Total Present, Absent, Pending).
  • 1-Click CSV Export for accreditation, administrative audits, and university compliance.

  ### 3. 🔗 Frictionless Link-Based Onboarding

  • Unique invite links for both teachers and students.
  • Built-in biometric photo capture & validation stored securely in Supabase storage for student identification.

  ### 4. 🤖 24/7 Groq-Powered AI Assistant

  • Embedded conversational AI agent directly in the dashboard.
  • Guides students and faculty through actions, timetable queries, and campus operations with zero learning
  curve.
