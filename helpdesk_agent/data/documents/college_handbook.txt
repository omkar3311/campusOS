## 1. College Information

**College Name:** Sunrise Institute of Technology and Management
**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra, India

Sunrise Institute of Technology and Management is a fictional college document created for testing the College Helpdesk RAG Agent.

The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, computer applications, management, and science.

## 2. Admissions

Admissions are handled by the **Central Admissions Office** located in the **Main Administrative Building, Ground Floor**.

Students who want to apply for admission should first complete the online application form and then visit the Central Admissions Office for document verification.

### Admission Process

The general admission process is:

1. Complete the online application form.
2. Upload the required academic documents.
3. Wait for the application to be reviewed.
4. Visit the Central Admissions Office for document verification.
5. Pay the applicable admission and tuition fees.
6. Collect the admission confirmation receipt.

Students should bring the following documents for verification:

* Government-issued identity proof
* Passport-size photographs
* Class 10 marksheet
* Class 12 marksheet
* Transfer certificate
* Migration certificate, if applicable
* Entrance examination scorecard, if applicable
* Caste/category certificate, if applicable
* Valid income certificate, if applicable
